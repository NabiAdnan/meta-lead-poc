Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Meta Lead Ads PoC demo handles information submitted through its test lead forms.

Information Collected

The application may receive your name, email address, phone number, submission time, and form identifier, depending on the fields included in the form.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to test Meta lead retrieval and demonstrate real-time delivery to a mobile dashboard. It is not intended for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Storage and Sharing

The demo backend temporarily holds received leads in memory and sends them to connected demo clients. Data may be lost when the backend restarts. Do not submit sensitive or confidential data.

Meta processes information according to its own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data Deletion

For questions or requests to delete information associated with a test submission, contact the developer using the email below.
